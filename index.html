✦ A little about me

Nice to meet you

I'm Emmanuel — an architectural designer who believes a home should feel as good as it looks.

I studied Architectural Technology at the Federal Polytechnic Nasarawa, earning my National Diploma and Higher National Diploma (2019–2024). For the past 2–3 years I've been designing homes for families from my base in Abuja and Kaduna, and working with clients around the world.

What I enjoy most is the moment a client sees their home in 3D for the first time and says, "That's exactly it." I'll listen closely, explain everything in plain language, and make sure you're confident before anything gets built.

✦ How I can help

What I can do for you

Whether you're starting from an empty plot or already have a plan, I can help you at any stage.

Home design

We'll plan your rooms, layout and look together — bungalows, duplexes and penthouses designed around your family, budget and plot.

Great if: you have land and an idea, but no plan yet.

Building drawings

Clear, detailed plans, sections and elevations prepared in Revit and AutoCAD — ready for approval and for your builder on site.

Great if: you're ready to get approval and start building.

3D renders & walkthroughs

Photo-realistic images and video walkthroughs in Enscape and Twinmotion, so you can see your home — day or night — before it's built.

Great if: you already have a plan and want to see it come alive.

Quick answers
Do you work outside Abuja and Kaduna?
